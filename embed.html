How to use it

  • Paste the snippet into a blog post, a design-system doc, or an accessibility checklist page.
  • The widget is 420px wide at most and 300px tall; it scales down on mobile.
  • Results update as the visitor types or picks a color — nothing is sent to a server.
  • Keep the attribution line: it is the only condition for using the embed.

What it checks

The widget computes the WCAG 2.2 contrast ratio with the relative-luminance formula and reports the three thresholds that matter in practice: AA normal text (4.5:1), AA large text (3:1), and AAA normal text (7:1). For the full tool — with suggestions that reach a target ratio and a UI-component check — use the contrast checker.
