Understanding WCAG Contrast Standards

📏 What is Contrast Ratio?

Contrast ratio measures the difference in luminance between two colors. The scale ranges from 1:1 (same color) to 21:1 (black on white). Higher ratios mean easier readability.

🏆 WCAG Levels Explained

  • AA Normal — 4.5:1 minimum. Standard for body text (< 18pt / < 14pt bold).
  • AA Large — 3:1 minimum. For large text (≥ 18pt / ≥ 14pt bold).
  • AAA Normal — 7:1 minimum. Enhanced accessibility for body text.
  • AAA Large — 4.5:1 minimum. Enhanced for large text.

🔤 What Counts as "Large Text"?

WCAG defines large text as at least 18pt (24px) regular weight, or 14pt (18.66px) bold. If text is below that threshold, it falls under "Normal" requirements.

💡 Pro Tips for Better Contrast

  • Dark text on light backgrounds is generally more readable than the reverse
  • Use the 60-30-10 rule: 60% dominant, 30% secondary, 10% accent color
  • Test on actual screens — different displays vary in brightness and calibration
  • Don't rely on color alone to convey information (e.g., use icons + color for status)
