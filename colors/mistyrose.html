#FFE4E1 — Misty Rose

Misty Rose is the CSS named color #FFE4E1 — rgb(255, 228, 225), hsl(6, 100%, 94%). Its relative luminance is 0.822, giving 1.20:1 against white and 17.44:1 against black, so it works as a text color on black.
