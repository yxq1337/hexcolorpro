#FFB6C1 — Light Pink

Light Pink is the CSS named color #FFB6C1 — rgb(255, 182, 193), hsl(351, 100%, 86%). Its relative luminance is 0.586, giving 1.65:1 against white and 12.71:1 against black, so it works as a text color on black.

Is lightpink light or dark?

Can I use lightpink for text?
