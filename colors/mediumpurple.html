#9370DB — Medium Purple

Medium Purple is the CSS named color #9370DB — rgb(147, 112, 219), hsl(260, 60%, 65%). Its relative luminance is 0.229, giving 3.76:1 against white and 5.58:1 against black, so it works as a text color on black.
