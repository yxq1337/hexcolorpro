#FF4500 — Orange Red

Orange Red is the CSS named color #FF4500 — rgb(255, 69, 0), hsl(16, 100%, 50%). Its relative luminance is 0.255, giving 3.44:1 against white and 6.10:1 against black, so it works as a text color on black.
