#66CDAA — Medium Aquamarine

Medium Aquamarine is the CSS named color #66CDAA — rgb(102, 205, 170), hsl(160, 51%, 60%). Its relative luminance is 0.494, giving 1.93:1 against white and 10.88:1 against black, so it works as a text color on black.
