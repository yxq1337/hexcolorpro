#F0FFF0 — Honeydew

Honeydew is the CSS named color #F0FFF0 — rgb(240, 255, 240), hsl(120, 100%, 97%). Its relative luminance is 0.963, giving 1.04:1 against white and 20.27:1 against black, so it works as a text color on black.
