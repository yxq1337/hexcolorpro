#48D1CC — Medium Turquoise

Medium Turquoise is the CSS named color #48D1CC — rgb(72, 209, 204), hsl(178, 60%, 55%). Its relative luminance is 0.513, giving 1.86:1 against white and 11.27:1 against black, so it works as a text color on black.
