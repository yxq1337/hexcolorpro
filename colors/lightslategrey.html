#778899 — Light Slate Grey

Light Slate Grey is the CSS named color #778899 — rgb(119, 136, 153), hsl(210, 14%, 53%). Its relative luminance is 0.238, giving 3.64:1 against white and 5.77:1 against black, so it works as a text color on black.
