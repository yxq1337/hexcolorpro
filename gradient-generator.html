How to Use CSS Gradients

📐 Linear Gradients

The most common type. Colors transition along a straight line. Use the angle slider to rotate the direction.

  • to right — left to right (equivalent to 90°)
  • to bottom right — diagonal gradient
  • 135deg — precise angle control

⭕ Radial Gradients

Colors transition outward from a center point. Great for backgrounds, buttons, and spotlight effects.

🎠 Conic Gradients

Colors transition around a center point (like a color wheel). Perfect for pie charts, color wheels, and creative effects.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Use 3-5 color stops for a rich, natural gradient
  • Adjust stop positions to control where each color starts and ends
  • Pair with the Color Picker to find the perfect starting colors
  • Use subtle gradients (similar hues) for backgrounds, bold ones for accents
